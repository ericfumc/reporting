Rules (draft)

      

      
    
        
  • Discrete: Block 1 and Block 2: ABC → (I-1)&(I-2):  ABC.
    • 
        
  • Range: Block 3 to 6 - or Block 3 to Block 6: → (I-3)-(I-6):  EFG.
    • 
        
  • Each line ends with .
    • 
        
  • Description may follow : or -; “Block” before the second number is optional
    • 
        
  • Descriptions aligned; shortest gap after : is two spaces
    • 
        
  • Designation - or blank → (1)&(2) (no letter)
    •
